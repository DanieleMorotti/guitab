Drag across bars to select them; with Loop on, the selection repeats (no selection: the whole song).
Metronome
A click on every beat while playing, at the tab's tempo.
Count-in
One bar of clicks before playback starts (at 80 BPM about 3 s).
Speed
Slower playback for practice; the pitch does not change.
